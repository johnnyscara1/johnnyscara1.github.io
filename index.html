Python · Exploratory Data Analysis

MLB Umpire Performance Analysis

Explored MLB umpire scorecard data from 2015–2022 to investigate home-team favor, changes in umpire accuracy, and differences in umpire impact across teams.

Used Python, pandas, and Matplotlib to clean missing values, prepare 18,093 game records, summarize trends, and create visualizations. Average umpire accuracy in the dataset increased from approximately 90.5% in 2015 to 93.8% in 2022.

New England Patriots Ticket Sales & Attendance

Examined relationships between ticket prices, attendance, team payroll, and winning percentage across Patriots seasons from 2006–2024, excluding 2020.

Compared correlation strengths to explore how affordability, team spending, and performance relate to attendance. The report discusses observed associations and their possible implications for fan engagement.
