Refined EDA Questions:¶

Using this dataset, I will answer these EDA questions:

  • Do umpires tend to favor the home team over the away team?
  • Has umpire performance improved or declined from 2015–2022?
  • Which specific teams have benefited the most and least from umpire performance over time?
